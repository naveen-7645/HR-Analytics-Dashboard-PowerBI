## Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard analyzes employee attrition using the IBM HR Analytics dataset.

## Tools Used
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modeling

## Key Metrics
- Total Employees: 1,470
- Employees Left: 237
- Attrition Rate: 16.12%
- Average Age: 37
- Average Tenure: 7 Years

## Dashboard Features
- Attrition by Department
- Attrition by Job Role
- Attrition by Gender
- Attrition by Age Group
- Attrition by Income Group
- Attrition by Education Field
- Attrition by Overtime
- Attrition by Business Travel
- Attrition by Job Satisfaction

## Key Insights
- Research & Development department has the highest attrition.
- Employees aged 26-35 show the highest attrition.
- Overtime employees are more likely to leave.
- Low-income employees have higher attrition.

## Dataset
IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset
